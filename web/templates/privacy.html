Privacy

Quarry has no accounts. Most of what you do stays in your browser.

Stored in your browser

  • Your profile (skills, filters, saved and dismissed programs) and a random anonymous id, in local storage.
  • Scope exports and the "For you" ranking are computed on your device. Nothing is uploaded for them.

Sent to the server only when you use these

  • Chat, "Ask about this program", smart search: your question and, for search, your profile, plus the anonymous id.
  • Scope check: the hosts you paste, to compare against a program's scope. They are not stored.
  • Save / dismiss: the program and reason, to tune your ranking.
  • Alerts: your Telegram chat id, Discord webhook URL or push subscription, whichever you set up.

A bot check (Cloudflare Turnstile) may run before chat to limit abuse. Aggregate, cookie-free visit counts may be collected with Cloudflare Web Analytics.

Delete your data

Use "Delete my data" on the Alerts page. It wipes everything held under your anonymous id on the server, removes subscriptions, and clears this browser's saved profile. You can also clear this site's storage in your browser settings.
